    How the Deal Score works

    Each deal gets 0 to 100 points. A "% off" banner earns nothing on its own: the discount is measured against what the item usually sells for, and inflated reference prices lose points.

    • Discount (35): % below the usual price. 40% or more earns the full 35. Measured only against list price when no usual price was found, at half credit.
    • Price history (15): at or below the lowest recent price = 15, within 5% = 10, within 15% = 5, unknown = 5.
    • Quality (25): star rating pulled toward 4.0 when there are few reviews ((n×rating + 50×4.0) ÷ (n + 50)); 4.8+ earns 25. A named expert pick adds 3.
    • Fit (15): priority sections (unique, outdoors, tech) start at 12, perfume and clothing at 4; +3 when it suits a likely recipient.
    • Coupon and confidence (10): +5 for an extra code or stack; +5 when 2+ sources confirm the price.
    • Penalties: −10 inflated "was" price, −5 third-party or gray-market seller.
